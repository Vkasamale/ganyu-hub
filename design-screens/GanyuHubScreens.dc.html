Ganyu Hub · product screens

Eight screens, two widths.

Every screen below is drawn at 390 and 1440 from the shipped code in Vkasamale/ganyu-hub — the same layouts, spacing, copy and money maths that run in the product. White ground, off-white raised surfaces, light grey for stamps and insets, Inter throughout. The five money stamps and nothing yet are supplied artwork, never redrawn.

Four things actually work: the landing hero switches audience, browse switches scope, the message list switches thread, and the job detail advances from held to released.

Screen 01

Signed-out landing

Proves the two-mode hero over photography: the image runs the full width behind the search and the category list, scrimmed ink 86% to 58% so the type holds. Five drop-in slots crossfade every six seconds with no controls. Press either pill to switch audience — the photographs stay put while the ground goes from ink to true black.
