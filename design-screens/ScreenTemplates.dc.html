Five layouts, two widths.

Every future mockup starts here. The ground is pure white #FFFFFF; off-white #F7F6F3 is the raised surface; light grey #ECECEC is the accent, carrying the stamp, empty states, and inset panels inside cards. Because the ground-to-card step is small, separation is carried by shadow and hairline.

Settled

The rubber stamp

A heavy, hand-pressed stamp — not a badge and not a chip. The stamps are supplied artwork, not drawn in code: a double ring carrying Ganyu Hub arced above and below, three stars each side, and an angled label band naming the state. Worn edges, uneven ink, every one different. Six exist: one per stage of a job's money, plus nothing yet for empty states. The five money stamps are never borrowed for absence, and no stamp appears anywhere outside those two jobs.

Earlier chip variants, and the coded imitation that followed them, were both replaced by this artwork on 20 August 2026. The files live in assets/stamps/.

All five money states

The five stamps mark the stages a job's money passes through, and they are the artwork itself — pressed, worn, each one different. Keyed to transparent so they sit on any ground, and cut from 2048px to 512px: the set is now about a tenth of the weight it arrived at, which matters on a paid connection.

Template 5

Empty state

No illustration. The panel is light grey with a dashed edge and carries the nothing yet stamp — its own artwork, never one of the five money stamps, which name stages of money that has not started moving here. Two weights: a whole empty surface gets the stamp and a way out, while one empty region on a page that is otherwise full gets a line of text and nothing else.
