Skills, tags, anything list-shaped a creative types themselves.

```jsx
<TagInput name="skills" defaultValue={["Logo design"]} placeholder="Logo design, packaging…" />
```

Committed chips are solid teal with paper text — the one place teal is used as a fill at small size, because a chip is a thing you made, not a link. Enter or comma commits; Backspace on an empty field removes the last chip. The hint line below is part of the component: nobody guesses "press Enter".
