A read-only pill. Categories, skills, counts, states. Never attach a click handler — a badge that acts should be a Button.

```jsx
<Badge tone="wash">Design</Badge>
<Badge tone="chip">Logo design</Badge>
<Badge tone="mark">Budget: MWK 120,000</Badge>
```

`mark` is the reserved green (#2F5D3B at 10%) and is only for money-adjacent facts and the verified badge. Money *state* is not a badge — it is `MoneyStamp`.
